About SpamShield
Deep learning powered email classification using Bidirectional LSTM.
What is SpamShield?

SpamShield is a full-stack web application that uses a Bidirectional LSTM (BiLSTM) deep learning model to classify emails as spam or legitimate (ham).

Unlike rule-based spam filters, the BiLSTM model learns patterns from real email data — understanding context, word order, and subtle linguistic cues.

Trained on thousands of real-world messages, achieving high precision and recall on complex spam patterns.

FAQ
Why BiLSTM instead of BERT?
With standard spam datasets, BERT often overfits due to its massive parameter count. BiLSTM is the sweet spot — powerful enough for context, small enough to generalize.
Is email data stored?
Yes — predictions are stored locally in history.db. Nothing is sent to external servers beyond your own instance.
